About MTL Legal

A result-oriented law firm offering a wide range of legal services to institutions, corporates and individuals, with particular expertise in banking & finance, litigation, arbitration, real estate and corporate advisory.

Who we are

Trusted counsel across transactions, disputes and advisory matters

MTL Legal acts as trusted legal counsel to clients on a broad spectrum of matters, including complex multi-party loan agreements, negotiations, dispute management and regulatory-facing matters.

The firm works with large corporate groups, banks, financial institutions, state government bodies, small and medium-sized businesses and start-ups. Whether the mandate is a syndicated facility, a title investigation across state lines or a long-running commercial dispute, the same principle applies: the advice has to work commercially, not only on paper.

Our approach combines legal rigour with commercial understanding. We focus on delivering effective, practical and responsive legal solutions aligned with our clients' business objectives, and we assist clients in navigating the evolving Indian legal environment.

Institutions & lenders

The apex bank (RBI), public and private sector banks, NBFCs, ARCs, financial institutions and trusteeship companies.

Corporates & promoters

Large corporate groups, borrowers, developers, construction companies, landlords and tenants, investors and shareholders.

Public bodies & growth businesses

State government bodies, small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups building in India.
