About the firm

Trusted counsel on transactions, disputes and regulatory matters

MTL Legal acts as trusted legal counsel to clients on a broad spectrum of transactions, disputes and advisory matters — including complex multi-party loan agreements, negotiations, dispute management and regulatory-facing matters.

The firm works with large corporate groups, banks, financial institutions, state government bodies, small and medium-sized businesses and start-ups. Our approach combines legal rigour with commercial understanding, and we focus on delivering effective, practical and responsive solutions aligned with our clients' business objectives.
