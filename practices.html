Practice Areas

Four connected practices, built around the transactions, disputes and regulatory questions our clients face in India — and staffed by lawyers who have handled them first-hand for decades.

01 Practice

Banking & Finance and Infrastructure

Our lawyers advise the apex bank (RBI), public and private sector banks and non-banking financial institutions.

Our Banking and Finance team has extensive experience and unsurpassed knowledge of the complexities of the Indian banking and financial sector. We provide legal advice on all types of financing.

  • Project financing
  • Corporate financing
  • Acquisition financing
  • Asset financing
  • Syndicated and bilateral loans
  • Project structuring
  • Due diligence
  • Securitization

Beyond the facility agreement

The practice extends to infrastructure and renewable energy projects, structured lending, NCD issuances, debt capital market transactions, multi-lender structures, security creation, regulatory compliance, stressed assets, debt restructuring and the enforcement of security interests.

02 Practice

Real Estate & Construction

We regularly represent lenders, corporates, landlords, tenants, construction companies and developers in commercial, industrial and residential real estate matters.

Our professionals provide comprehensive and commercially driven legal advice across all stages of real estate and construction transactions, with experience across multiple states.

  • Legal due diligence
  • Title investigation
  • Transaction structuring
  • Transfer and conveyancing
  • Adjudication
  • Registration
  • Joint development & collaboration
  • Sale and lease arrangements

Adjudication and Registration

We specialise in the adjudication and registration of financial and property-related documents, including financing and security documents. We provide these services across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka and Delhi, and also assist in entering clients' names in revenue records.

03 Practice

Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Our lawyers have long been recognised as industry leaders in India, with significant experience in factually and legally complex disputes across all fields of commercial law.

Our success in dispute resolution is strongly supported by our lawyers' in-depth understanding of governing laws, commercial customs and business culture in India.

Our litigation lawyers have extensive experience representing clients in the Supreme Court, High Courts and Civil Courts, in arbitration, and before quasi-judicial authorities and tribunals including Debts Recovery Tribunals and National Company Law Tribunals.

  • Commercial litigation
  • Domestic & international arbitration
  • Banking & recovery litigation
  • Insolvency before the NCLT
  • Construction contract disputes
  • Criminal litigation
  • Corporate frauds & economic offences
  • Securities law violations
04 Practice

Corporate Advisory

Transactional and day-to-day corporate counsel for domestic and international clients, financial institutions, investors, developers and government bodies.

We advise on complex transactions across real estate, infrastructure, hospitality, warehousing and renewable energy sectors, and on the corporate arrangements that sit behind them.

  • Shareholders' agreements
  • Business & collaboration agreements
  • Acquisitions & investment transactions
  • Capital raising
  • Foreign investment
  • Acquisition of distressed assets
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Secretarial & governance matters

Also within the practice

Intellectual property rights, legal audits for lenders, and transaction documentation for banks, financial institutions, NBFCs and ARCs.
